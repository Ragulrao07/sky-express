Frequently Asked Questions

How does the booking request process work?

When you fill out the booking form, we receive your preferred date, location, and service requirements. Because equipment and technician availability must be verified, we will contact you via WhatsApp or phone to confirm the schedule.

Which areas do you cover for on-site services?

We are based in Chow Kit, Kuala Lumpur, and service Kuala Lumpur and surrounding central areas.

Can I contact you directly without filling the form?
